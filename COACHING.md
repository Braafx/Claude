## 6-Month Progression Roadmap

### Month 1–2: Lock in the Process (Foundation Phase)

**Goal:** Have a repeatable digital painting process you don't have to think about.

**Weekly structure:**
- Mon/Tue evening: studies (values, materials, lighting variations)
- Thu/Fri evening: `/draw` brief — one completed piece or near-complete study
- Weekend: one longer session (2–3 hrs) on a portfolio-intent piece

**Skills to drill:**
- Value block-in (greyscale only, 3 values max) — 10 studies
- Material differentiation: same form, 4 different materials — 8 studies
- Edge variation: hard vs soft — 5 focused exercises
- The `/draw` process skeleton every digital session, no exceptions

**Milestones:**
- [ ] Complete 8 digital studies using the 5-step process

### Content mix per week (art account)

```
Mon: Finished piece or polished WIP
Wed: Process breakdown / step-by-step (these get saves)
Fri: Sketchbook page, quick sketch, or personal piece
Weekend (optional): Meme recreation or game throwback art
```

### Content types that grow art accounts

1. **Process posts** — "sketch to render" carousels perform well. Screenshot each stage.
2. **Meme recreations** — take a well-known meme format, redraw it in your style. The contrast between "serious art skill" and "dumb meme" is the joke and the algorithm likes it.
3. **Game throwbacks** — draw a character or scene inspired by a beloved game (Sly, R&C, etc.) and tag the original game. Community reposts.
4. **Finished character or prop** — clear subject, strong composition, good lighting. Put your best work forward.
5. **"Drawing X in Y style"** — pick a character and draw them in a different studio's style. High engagement.

## The Long Game: Riot Games Path

### What Riot looks for (junior → mid concept artist)

- Strong fundamentals: value, form, light, perspective
- A clear personal style that still works within briefs
- Ability to design to a theme/brief (not just "cool-looking stuff")
- ArtStation presence with 6–10 polished, varied pieces
- Evidence of visual storytelling: characters that have a world, props that have a history

### Your competitive advantages to build

1. **Game culture depth** — you grew up with these games. You understand the emotional language of PS2-era mascots and what makes them timeless. That cultural fluency shows in design decisions.
2. **Daily practice discipline** — you already have the habit. Most people don't. The habit wins long races.
3. **Systems thinking** — you built a whole workspace to optimise your learning. That meta-skill makes you faster at everything else.
4. **Authentic voice** — the stone cube piece has personality. It's not generic. Your work has a POV.

### Riot portfolio targets (what they want to see)

- 2+ character designs with clear personality, faction, and silhouette
- 1+ environment piece with mood, depth, and narrative
- 1+ prop/weapon sheet with material callouts
- Evidence of iteration: show 5 thumbnails and the chosen direction
- A splash-art style character piece: full body, dynamic pose, cinematic lighting
